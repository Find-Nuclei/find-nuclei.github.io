On this page

Local Files ​

Open OME-ZARR data from your local disk without uploading anything.

Reading from disk is the fastest and most private way to work, but it is not the only one. Your OME-ZARR Storage Setup Is Slowing You Down compares this with streaming from a server, and is worth reading before a lab or facility commits to one setup.

Drag and Drop ​

The simplest way to open local data is drag and drop:

  1. Open Find Nuclei Viewer
  2. Drag your .ome.zarr folder from Finder/Explorer onto the browser
  3. Drop it. The viewer opens immediately

Using the File Picker ​

Alternatively, use the built-in folder picker (Chrome and Edge only):

  1. Click Open Image on the landing page
  2. Click Open Folder in the Load Data modal
  3. Select your .ome.zarr folder
  4. The viewer loads your data

Browser Support

The folder picker uses the File System Access API, which is only available in Chrome and Edge. For other browsers, use drag and drop.

Privacy ​

Your data stays entirely on your machine. Find Nuclei Viewer processes everything locally in the browser. No files are uploaded to any server.

Recent Images ​

After opening a dataset, it appears in your Recently Opened list on the landing page. Click any recent image to reopen it instantly. Thumbnails and metadata are cached in your browser's local storage.
