On this page

Guides ​

Detailed guides for getting the most out of Find Nuclei Viewer.

Opening Data ​

  • Supported Formats: every format the viewer opens, in one table
  • Pixel Size and Magnification: set them when a file leaves them out or gets them wrong

Where Your Data Lives ​

  • Local Files: Open a file or a folder from your disk, in any format, with nothing uploaded
  • Remote URLs: Open an image by its address: https://, s3:// or gs://
  • Private Images and Slides: Open private images and slides with a private link or a token. For developers: AWS SigV4 and custom signers
  • Data Server: Host your own on-site ZARR server with Docker

Microscopy ​

  • OME-Zarr: NGFF 0.3 to 0.6, Zarr v2 and v3, plates and labels, by address, folder or one zipped file
  • OME-TIFF: Pyramids, channels, Z and time, and 3D for a Z-stack
  • Zeiss CZI Files: Open a CZI from ZEN by its address or from your disk, including Celldiscoverer 7 plates

High-Content Screening ​

  • OME-Zarr Plates: Navigate high-content screening plate data, and open any field in 3D
  • Opera Phenix Plates: Open an Opera Phenix or Harmony plate from its index, on the web or on disk
  • Yokogawa CellVoyager Plates: Open a CellVoyager CV8000 or CV7000 plate from its measurement folder, on the web or on disk

Digital Pathology ​

  • Aperio SVS: Aperio slides with label and macro
  • Hamamatsu NDPI: NanoZoomer slides, over 4 GB too
  • Leica SCN: Every scanned region, brightfield and fluorescence
  • Philips TIFF: Philips slides with label and macro
  • Akoya QPTIFF: Brightfield and multiplex fluorescence with marker names, plus inForm exports
  • 3DHISTECH MRXS: Pannoramic slides from a folder or a zip
  • DICOM WSI: A folder of .dcm files or a zip

Viewing and Display ​

  • Channels: Pick colors, adjust contrast, auto-contrast, gamma, RGB/BW/INV display modes
  • Z-Projection: Flatten a Z-stack into one image with maximum, mean or minimum intensity
  • 3D Rendering: View a Z-stack as a WebGPU volume with MIP or Composite, next to orthogonal XY, XZ and YZ planes, and a Region for one area in full detail
  • 3D Region: Draw a box on any plane and see that part alone in 3D at the finest detail that fits, for a very large image or to look inside a tissue

Segmentation and Analysis ​

  • Example: Plate with Results: Open a 96-well screen with its masks and measurements in one click
  • Labels: Load segmentation masks, control display, add metadata, organise by source or key-value
  • Analytics & Phenotyping: Load CSVs, color by column, filter, and build named cell populations
  • Annotations: Draw, select, bulk-edit, and classify regions of interest
  • Exchanging Annotations: Import from QuPath, Visiopharm, ImageScope and NDP.view, export GeoJSON and OME-XML, share a link with the annotations
  • Annotation Templates: Create reusable template presets for color + class + keyboard shortcut

Sharing ​

  • Sharing Views: Share your exact view with a URL
  • Embedding in Publications: Add an interactive viewer to any web page with two lines of HTML
  • Reader Analytics: See how readers use the figures you embed, with your own analytics

Reference ​

  • Sample Data and Credits: Where the images in these guides come from, and how to cite them
  • Analytics Data Format: Publish measurements and masks so the viewer loads them from one URL
