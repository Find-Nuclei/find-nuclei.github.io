Video script: Annotations ​

Target length: ~90 seconds Audience: Pathologists / cell biologists evaluating the viewer Call to action: Open the demo, try the Select tool, draw something

The existing YouTube intro (Local Files, HCS Plates) is the template: tight voiceover, fast cuts, no jargon in the first 10 seconds.

Cold open (0:00 – 0:08) ​

Visual: WSI loaded in viewer. A handful of pre-drawn annotations: three tumor polygons, two stroma, a necrosis region. Labels visible.

Voiceover:

"Annotations in Find Nuclei Viewer. Draw regions, classify them, and edit hundreds at once."

Draw a polygon (0:08 – 0:20) ​

Visual:

  • Cursor moves to the Polygon tool in the toolbar. Click.
  • Cursor returns to the canvas. Click-click-click-click around a cell cluster. Double-click to close.
  • A red polygon fills the region.

Voiceover:

"Click to place vertices. Double-click to close. Or press-and-drag for freehand. The path is simplified on release, so a scribble becomes a clean polygon."

Optional cut: show the freehand version on a second region (2 seconds).

Templates for fast classification (0:20 – 0:35) ​

Visual:

  • On-canvas template chip row appears while polygon tool is active: "Tumor", "Stroma", "Necrosis", "Immune" colored chips.
  • Click "Stroma". The chip lights up green.
  • Draw another polygon. It automatically fills green and labels as "Stroma 3".

Voiceover:

"Templates pre-set the color and class. Pick one, and every polygon you draw next inherits it: color, class, sequential label, all in one click. Alt-1 through 9 for keyboard shortcuts."

Select and reshape (0:35 – 0:50) ​

Visual:

  • Switch to Select tool (V key overlay shown briefly).
  • Click a polygon. Vertex handles appear as white dots.
  • Drag a handle outward. The polygon reshapes live.
  • Drag a midpoint. A new vertex appears.
  • Press Escape.

Voiceover:

"Select mode shows vertex handles. Drag any corner to reshape. Drag between corners to add a new vertex. Polygons also support freehand reshape. Hold a corner and sketch a new arc."

Multi-select and lasso (0:50 – 1:05) ​

Visual:

  • Cmd+click two rows in the annotation list. Green borders appear, bulk action bar slides in showing "2 selected".
  • Switch to Select tool on canvas. Draw a loop around a cluster. Dashed green lasso path.
  • Release. Five annotations get green halos, list shows "5 selected".
  • Bulk bar: pick "Tumor" from the Template dropdown. Every selected annotation turns red, reclassifies.

Voiceover:

"Cmd-click the list to multi-select. Or drag a freehand lasso on canvas. Anything fully inside joins the selection. Then bulk apply a template, change color, hide, or delete. All in one undoable step."

Group move + cleanup (1:05 – 1:20) ​

Visual:

  • Drag one of the still-selected polygons; all five move together.
  • Press arrow keys. The group nudges by one pixel.
  • Shift+arrow jumps 10 pixels.
  • Press Escape. Selection clears. Halos fade.

Voiceover:

"Grab any selected shape and the whole group follows. Arrow keys nudge by a pixel. Shift-arrow jumps ten. Escape to clear."

Close (1:20 – 1:30) ​

Visual: Zoom out to full tissue. All annotations colored by class (tumor red, stroma green, necrosis yellow). Legend card visible bottom-right.

Voiceover:

"Works on any OME-Zarr image. Saves to your browser. Share a URL and a reviewer opens the same view. Try it at find-nuclei dot github dot io."

On-screen text: find-nuclei.github.io

Production notes ​

  • Screen capture resolution: 1920×1080, retina source preferred.
  • Cursor: show the OS cursor, bump its size one step above default.
  • Keypresses: show a key-cap overlay for V, Esc, Cmd-click, Shift-click. The YouTube reference videos use screen-studio.com-style key caps.
  • Music: none or very quiet. The Local Files video has no music. Keep consistent.
  • Pace: aim for 4–5 cuts per 30 seconds. Never hold a shot more than 5 seconds without a cursor movement or UI change.
  • Thumbnail candidate: the multi-select halo shot (0:55 area). It's visually distinctive.
