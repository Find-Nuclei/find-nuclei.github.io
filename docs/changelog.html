2026-11-25 ​

  • The 3D tools moved to a rail on the right. The panels gain the height of the old toolbar, and nothing covers the image while you move around. Rest the pointer on the rail to see the names. Annotate opens the drawing tools at the top of the panels. Layout, View and Reset are on the status bar, next to Scale and Background. Watch the demo
  • The 2D | 3D switch is at the top of the side panel, above Navigation and View Options, so it is one click away from either. Leave 3D, and you are on the tab you were on there.
  • Filter and Search show in the Channels panel with more than 8 channels.
  • Olympus VSI images open in more cases. A .vsi that holds its picture itself opens on its own. A renamed .vsi opens with the one data folder beside it. The data folder alone says which folder to open. Olympus VSI
  • Several labels on a plate's tiles. Tick up to four in the Label menu, such as Well ID, Compound and Dose. Each takes a line, a number shows its unit, and the text fits its tile. Labelling the tiles
  • SVS slides from any scanner, and TIFF slides with a pyramid. A .svs that another scanner or program saved opens as an Aperio one does. A TIFF with a thumbnail or label between its resolutions opens too.
  • 3D gives back the memory of the image you leave. Going through many large stacks in 3D no longer fills the memory of your computer.
  • Open an example from a format guide. The first button opens a file of that format, the next one your own data.
  • A slide's Label and Macro on iPhone open over the whole screen, with the buttons to turn and close them.
  • Help when 3D stops. If your graphics card keeps dropping out of 3D, the image opens in 2D and a message links the settings that can turn 3D on.
  • A short note for Leica projects. When many images of a project cannot be shown, the note says how many, and Image Information lists them.
  • Help when a file does not open. The message stays until you close it, with a link to the guide for that format and one to write to us. In Firefox and Safari, which open no folders, it says how to open an image saved as a folder, such as a VSI, an MRXS slide, an OME-Zarr or a plate, and how to zip it.
  • OME-TIFF: JPEG slides in their own colours, and small JPEG 2000 levels that stayed empty. Some OME-TIFF slides written by Bio-Formats opened in false colours, and the minimap of some JPEG 2000 files was blank.
  • Channel histograms show on 8-bit images with few intensities. On these the histogram in the Channels panel was empty.
