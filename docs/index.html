Try the Viewer

Local Files

Drag and drop OME-Zarr folders from your disk. Your data never leaves your machine. Every pixel is processed locally in the browser.

Remote URLs

Paste any public OME-Zarr URL and stream tiles directly from the server. No download required.

HCS Plates

Open any HCS plate. 96, 384, or 1536-well. Zoom seamlessly from plate overview to individual cells.

Share with a Link

Copy a shareable URL that captures your exact view. Zoom, position, channels, and colors. Google Maps-style deep links.

Multi-Channel & Auto-Contrast

Custom channel colors, per-channel histograms, gamma, and one-click percentile-based auto-contrast. GPU-accelerated blending.

Segmentation Overlays

Embedded OME-Zarr labels or linked external stores. Per-object coloring, tags, key-value metadata, drag-to-reorder.

Shape Annotations

Polygons, lines, points, and distances. Multi-select with a freehand lasso. Apply templates in bulk. Auto-saved locally. Export as CSV or GeoJSON.

Analytics & Phenotyping

Load a measurement CSV and color cells by any column. Filter objects by range. Build named cell populations with composable filter conditions. All client-side, data stays local.

Z-Projection

Max, mean, and min intensity projections with configurable Z-range for 3D datasets.
