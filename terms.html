2. Service Description

Find Nuclei Viewer is a browser-based visualization tool for OME-ZARR (OME-NGFF) microscopy data. The Service is currently in pre-release and is provided free of charge with certain limitations.

  • Browse High Content Screening (HCS) plate layouts
