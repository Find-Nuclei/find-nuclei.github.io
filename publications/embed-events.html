Listening on your own page

This is the whole listener. Send e.detail wherever your own tracking goes. Give each viewer an id, as this page does, and every event says which figure it came from. Every field is in the embedding guide. 

document.addEventListener('fn-viewer:interaction', (e) => {
  yourTracker.send(e.detail);
});
